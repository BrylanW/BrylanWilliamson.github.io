# Final Capstone Policy Memo — Kettering Health Cyberattack (Week 8)

## Overview
This page links to my final project for CYBR2100: the **Capstone Policy Memo**.  
In this project, I analyzed the May 2025 ransomware attack on Kettering Health, which disrupted hospital systems, delayed patient care, and forced a shift to manual operations.  

The memo includes a full incident analysis, legal and ethical evaluation, and a comprehensive Cybersecurity & Incident Governance Policy designed to strengthen healthcare system resilience and response capabilities.

## Reflection
This course brought together key concepts in cybersecurity, law, ethics, and real-world incident response.  
Working on this memo helped me understand how cyberattacks impact not just systems, but real people—especially in healthcare where delays can directly affect patient outcomes.  

One of the biggest takeaways was how critical preparation and clear governance are during a crisis.  
Things like access control, monitoring, and response procedures aren’t just technical safeguards—they determine how well an organization can continue operating under pressure.  

I also saw how small gaps, like weak segmentation or excessive user privileges, can escalate into large-scale disruptions when exploited.
